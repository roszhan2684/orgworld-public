# Code of Conduct

OrgWorld welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, give and accept constructive feedback, critique ideas rather than people, and assume good faith.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

**Scope:** issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile ([@roszhan2684](https://github.com/roszhan2684)). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, or warn, suspend or ban participants who break this code.
